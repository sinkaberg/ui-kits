How to use a kit

Link the versioned URL straight from your document — no copies in your repo:

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://sinkaberg.github.io/ui-kits/docs/v1/sinkaberg-docs.css">

The v1 path is the compatibility contract: it only ever receives compatible updates. Releases are tagged (docs/v1.x.y) with a line in the changelog. Keep a local clone as an offline fallback.
